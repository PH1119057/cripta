P48.4 CLOCK WATCHDOG + AUTOMATIC RECOVERY

Purpose
- Keep the read-only scanner alive during Windows/Bybit clock drift.
- Correct clock drift before it reaches the authenticated REST rejection zone.
- Never require a manual reconnect solely because of clock skew.

Changes
1. Clock watchdog interval: 60s -> 15s.
2. At |offset| >= 500 ms, perform up to 3 W32Time resync/re-measure cycles.
3. If the correction leaves |offset| between 500 and 750 ms, keep the session alive,
   remain fail-closed for unsafe conditions, and check again after 15 seconds.
4. If |offset| remains > 750 ms, close the current read-only session safely, resync,
   and automatically reconnect while the operator still wants the runtime connected.
5. Clock-skew recovery does not consume the finite transport reconnect budget; it is
   persistent because the screener must resume automatically after time recovery.
6. UI Clock tooltip documents the 15-second watchdog and automatic reconnect behavior.

Unchanged
- Entry/Exit strategy logic.
- Leverage/position sizing logic.
- Authentication/permission failures remain fail-closed and do not retry forever.
- Ordinary transport reconnect budget remains unchanged.
