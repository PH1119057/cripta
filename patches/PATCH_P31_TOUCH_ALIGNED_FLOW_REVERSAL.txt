P31 — TOUCH-ALIGNED FLOW REVERSAL RESEARCH

- Entry research only; live execution and exit logic are unchanged.
- Reuses the frozen P30 90-day dataset and retained Bybit public trade archives.
- Resolves the actual first touch inside each five-minute candidate bar from raw trades.
- Recomputes +0.5/-0.5 and +0.5/-1.0 ordering from the raw tape after the real touch.
- Aligns the flow-reversal window to the real touch, not to the five-minute bar open.
- Separates four-minute adverse pressure from the final completed one-minute reversal.
- Produces control groups, Long/Short splits, three 30-day slices and a threshold matrix.
- No pressure/reversal threshold becomes a trading gate in P31.
