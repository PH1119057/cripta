P53_1M_ENTRY_DISPLACEMENT_V1_5

Baseline:
- bybit-workbench 0.8.5 / P48.2
- accepted P53 V1.4 research overlay

Root cause fixed:
- V1.4 seeded the first 1m archive from five[0].open.
- frozen trade_5m.csv can contain warm-up candles earlier than the first public-trade archive.
- this selected a stale warm-up open (UNI 3.232) instead of the matching archive-boundary 5m open (UNI 3.483).

V1.5:
- derives the first public-trade archive day from the frozen dataset manifest;
- requires an exact frozen 5m candle at that day 00:00 UTC;
- uses only that candle open as the causal boundary seed;
- keeps exact 1m->5m OHLCV equivalence with no tolerance;
- keeps cache V3 and rebuilds only days whose cached first open conflicts with the corrected seed.

Does not change:
- frozen 1063 Entry cohort or Entry fingerprint;
- Entry/Exit/Risk/Execution/live/UI;
- NEW5/P46/holdout;
- market data or reports during installation.
