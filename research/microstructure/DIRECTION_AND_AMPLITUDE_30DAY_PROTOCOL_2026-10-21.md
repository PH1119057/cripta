# CRIPTA — отдельное исследование LONG/SHORT direction и силы движения

**RESEARCH DESIGN, 2026-10-21. NOT CANON / NOT STRATEGY / NOT RESEARCH-READY.** После ранее завершённого 30-дневного forward-return screen владелец запросил конкретную вероятность LONG/SHORT и амплитуду по каждой монете. Этот протокол фиксирует дополнительную диагностику **до** чтения её результатов; старые 6 факторов и train-only пороги q20/q80 не переобучаются по validation/holdout. Exact symbol scope: Bybit APTUSDT/INJUSDT/DOTUSDT/LTCUSDT/ARBUSDT. UTC pilot 2026-05-18..2026-06-16.

- Inputs: frozen per-symbol 6-factor physical cube and its 150 source-file hashes; primary factor IDs **1 RVOL, 2 Trade Rate, 3 Trade Rate Acceleration, 5 Delta, 6 UTC-day CVD, 7 Delta Acceleration**. Conditional buckets: HIGH `X>=q80(TRAIN)` and LOW `X<=q20(TRAIN)`; also all-eligible unconditioned baseline. Thresholds read **unchanged** from `FORWARD_RETURN_30DAY_SCREEN_{SYMBOL}_2026-10-21.json`. Do not invent normalized strength or automatic cross-coin ranking.
- Future *endpoint* label at h=5,15,60 completed minutes: `ret_bps=10000*ln(last_actual_trade_price[t+h]/last_actual_trade_price[t])`. `ret>0` means price up (would favor LONG gross); `ret<0` means down (SHORT gross). Magnitude labels `ret>=+20,+50,+100` bps or `ret<=-20,-50,-100` bps are **descriptive thresholds only**, not stops, TP, policy or expected execution.
- Intrahorizon realized price-extreme diagnostics over next `(t,t+h]` from **actual trade highs and lows in each minute**, not fillable order quotes: `MFE_LONG=10000*ln(max_next_print_price/P[t])`; `MAE_LONG=10000*ln(min_next_print_price/P[t])`. Similarly maximum downside `-MAE_LONG` is potential SHORT favorable, and MFE_LONG is potential SHORT adverse. Report median and p90 excursions, median and p90 absolute endpoint returns. No future extrema in features.
- Require all h future minute buckets have >=1 recorded trade and last-trade price at t+h available; no forward price interpolation. Compare within three **previously frozen** folds: TRAIN May18–Jun06, VALIDATION Jun07–Jun09, late HOLDOUT Jun10–Jun16. Purge h trailing anchors from each fold. Do not rely on individual overlapping-minute labels as independent draws; report number of **distinct holdout UTC days** with group samples and daily direction prevalence.
- For every symbol×factor×bucket×horizon (5×6×2×3=180) and unconditional coin×horizon×fold, report sample count, fraction up/down, fraction achieving +/−20/50/100 bps on the endpoint, median/p90 absolute return, median/p90 future maximum favorable long and short excursion, mean endpoint signed bps. Compare conditional probabilities with the same coin/horizon/fold baseline. No trade simulation or after-fee P&L.
- To make a direct yes/no diagnostic, a candidate high/low bucket's **direction** is frozen from its TRAIN uplift in achieving +20bps (LONG) vs −20bps (SHORT) **relative to the same TRAIN baseline**; if neither train uplift is positive it is `NONE`. Evaluate this fixed direction on validation and holdout, state holdout uplift and holdout sample size/day counts. Highlight only when (i) train, validation, holdout uplift all positive, (ii) holdout has >=100 events and >=5 separate UTC days; this is an **exploratory consistency filter, not statistical certification** and cannot be called a trading signal. Source clock `observed_at` is absent, so historic on-time availability is not established.
- Important distinction: a positive HIGH-LOW contrast `E[ret|HIGH]-E[ret|LOW]` can occur with **both groups falling**, so is not a LONG recommendation. Similarly empirical direction classification without fees, slippage, spread, capacity, future order fill and risk cannot establish usable P&L.
- QC: SHA256 all source archives vs registered manifest; exact source minute-fill count parity vs independently QC-passed physical cube; strict observed timestamp order; no high/low overlap when train thresholds equal (report undefined); independent consistency of endpoint signs, extrema, fold boundaries and train-only thresholds. Output read-only under actor `cripta` to stdout, version source + results in GitHub. No live/Strategy/DB/server filesystem writes.

This design is a **post-screening diagnostic**, hence all 180 candidate directions involve selection/multiple-testing risk. Genuine validation must follow on a subsequent independently QC'd period (2026-06-17..2026-07-16 manifest frozen but raw fill/feature QC incomplete) with no formula/threshold revisions and with a valid decision-time availability model.
